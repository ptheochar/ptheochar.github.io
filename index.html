About

I am an AI Engineer and researcher at the intersection of academic research and industry. My expertise lies in leading complex machine learning projects with specialisation in Large Language Models, RAG systems, and Natural Language Processing.

Holding a PhD from the University of Thessaly, I bridge theoretical research with practical applications, developing AI solutions for healthcare, precision medicine, and language-intensive systems.

My research focuses on applying deep learning to real-world biomedical problems — from topic modelling with LLMs to predictive models for precision medicine. I have contributed to nationally and EU-funded initiatives and maintain an active record of peer review across top-tier venues.
