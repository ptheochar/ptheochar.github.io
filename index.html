About#

I am an AI Engineer and researcher focused on the research-to-production pipeline. My expertise lies in architecting Large Language Models, RAG systems, and advanced NLP solutions that thrive in real-world environments.

Following my PhD at the University of Thessaly, I have dedicated my career to translating theoretical breakthroughs into production-ready software, particularly in healthcare and precision medicine.

My work at Covariance involves leading data science teams to build robust AI infrastructures. Simultaneously, I maintain a strong academic presence through peer review for top-tier venues and continued research into deep learning applications.
