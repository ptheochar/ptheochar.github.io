About

Research to production#

AI engineer and researcher focused on LLMs, RAG, and NLP that work in production—especially in healthcare and precision medicine.

After my PhD at the University of Thessaly, I focus on turning research into robust software: retrieval systems, model integration APIs, and analytics platforms used in the field.

At Covariance I lead data science for healthcare AI. I also stay active in academia through peer review and applied deep learning research.
