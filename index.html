Bio

I am a PhD Candidate at the University of Thessaly (UTH) in the Intelligent Systems Lab, with Prof. Vassilis Plagianakos. I completed my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science and Biomedical Informatics and a Master of Science in Computer Science and Computational Biomedicine at UTH, with a minor in Computational Medicine and Biology.

My research focuses on using deep learning technologies in order to design, study and develop efficient multilayer neural networks for a deeper understanding of natural language by combining meta-information from frequently occurring images and hyperlinks related to the texts. In addition, their characteristics will be studied and interpreted by these models, produced in their internal structure, throughout the training process. Finally, the performance improvement of trained models will be studied using augmented methods - continuing learning in new data in order to reduce their training time.
