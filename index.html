About Me

I am an AI Engineer and researcher driving innovation at the intersection of academic research and industry applications. My expertise lies in leading complex machine learning projects with specialization in Large Language Models (LLMs), RAG systems, and Natural Language Processing.


As a PhD candidate in Computer Science and Biomedical Informatics at the University of Thessaly, I bridge theoretical research with practical applications, developing state-of-the-art AI solutions for healthcare and precision medicine. I'm passionate about pushing technological boundaries through collaborative innovation.
